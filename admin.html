Shop Details

These appear in the header, hero banner and footer of your customer site.

Logo

Shown in the header, footer and About page of your customer site. Square images look best (it is displayed as a circle).
After changing, press "Save All Changes" to publish. Browser-tab icons are separate files.

Announcement Banner

A message across the top of your customer site, e.g. "Closed Sunday" or "Diwali orders close Nov 10".

Site Photos

Hero banner, story section, bulk/festival banner and the four category tiles.

Manage Products

Edit names, prices, photos — mark bestsellers, add new items, or remove old ones.

Customer Orders

Orders customers place after paying through Razorpay. Confirm each payment in your Razorpay dashboard, then mark it here.
