Our Story

Two Decades of Tirunelveli's Own Sweets

Vannai Sri Santhi Sweets & Bakery has been a fixture on Tirunelveli's sweet counters since 2004. What began as a small family kitchen has grown into a name locals trust for one simple reason — everything we sell, we make ourselves.

What We're Known For

We're best known for our Tirunelveli Halwa, Mixture and Balcova, made fresh in small batches every single day. Alongside these, our full range covers traditional Tamil Nadu sweets — Laddoo, Jilebi, Jangiri, Mysorepak, Milk Sweet, Badhusha and Burfi — and savouries including Mixture, Muruku, Pakoda, Thattai, Sev and Andhra Muruku.

Own-Made, Always

Nothing on our counter is store-bought or outsourced. No added preservatives, no shortcuts — just recipes perfected over the years and made the way they always have been.

Now Shipping Nationwide

What started as a Tirunelveli shop now ships across Tamil Nadu and all of India by courier, so families far from home can still taste something made the traditional way. We also take bulk and festival orders for weddings, Diwali and corporate gifting.
