Our Story

Two Decades of Tirunelveli's Own Sweets

Since 2004, Vannai Sri Santhi Sweets & Bakery has been a fixture on Tirunelveli's sweet counters — famous for our halwa, mixture and balcova, made fresh every single day in small batches. No shortcuts, no store-bought mixes, just recipes passed down and perfected over the years.

Today we ship our sweets and savouries across Tamil Nadu and all of India, so families far from home can still taste something made the way it always has been.
