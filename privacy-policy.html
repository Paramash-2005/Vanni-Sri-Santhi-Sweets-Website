Vannai Sri Santhi Sweets & Bakery ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order details: name, phone number, delivery address, and the items you order.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment gateway partner (e.g. Razorpay). We do not see or store your full card number, UPI PIN, or bank login details — these are handled entirely by the payment provider under their own security standards.
  • Contact details: if you reach out via our enquiry form, phone, or WhatsApp, we keep the details you share so we can respond.
  • Basic browsing data: standard technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used only to keep the site working properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order status.
  • To respond to bulk, festival, or general enquiries.
  • To improve our website and products.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing of Information

We share order and delivery details only with the courier partners who ship your order, and payment details only with our payment gateway provider, solely to complete your transaction.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to remember items in your basket and improve your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, write to us at care@vannaisrisanthi.com or call +91 98765 43210.
