Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [insert date before publishing]

Because our sweets and savouries are perishable, fresh, and made to order, all sales are final once an order has been shipped. We do not accept returns, replacements, or refunds after dispatch.

1. Order Cancellations

You may cancel your order free of charge within 2 hours of placing it, as long as preparation has not yet begun. To cancel within this window, contact us immediately by phone or WhatsApp with your order ID.

Once your order has moved to preparation or been packed for shipment, it can no longer be cancelled.

2. Returns & Refunds

Given the perishable nature of our products, we are unable to accept returns or issue refunds once an order has been shipped, including for reasons such as change of mind, taste preference, or delayed pickup.

3. Incorrect or Undelivered Orders

If your order was placed with an incorrect or incomplete delivery address, or if a delivery attempt fails due to the recipient being unavailable, any resulting delay or non-delivery is not eligible for a refund.

4. Payment Issues

If a payment is deducted but your order does not get confirmed on our website, please contact us with your payment reference so we can look into it. Any amount confirmed as wrongly charged will be reversed to your original payment method through our payment gateway partner.

5. Bulk & Festival Orders

Cancellation terms for bulk, wedding, or festival orders are confirmed individually at the time of booking, given the advance preparation involved.

6. Contact Us

For any order-related concerns, reach us at care@vannaisrisanthi.com or +91 98765 43210 during business hours.

Note: This reflects a strict no-returns policy for perishable goods, as specified. Please confirm the 2-hour cancellation window matches your actual kitchen workflow before publishing, and have this reviewed alongside your Terms & Conditions.
